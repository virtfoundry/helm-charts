# Sponsorship

VirtFoundry is free and open source (Apache 2.0). **Sponsorship** helps fund development and documentation — without selling the codebase or transferring project leadership.

## What sponsorship is

| Sponsorship | Commercial license / acquisition |
|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Voluntary financial support | Purchase of rights or company |
| Optional visibility (logo, thanks) | Contractual exclusivity |
| May include agreed consulting hours | Full control of the OSS project |

Sponsors **do not** automatically get:

- merge access to `main`
- veto power over roadmap or architecture
- ownership of code already released under Apache 2.0

The **maintainer** remains responsible for official releases and repository decisions unless a separate contract says otherwise.

## Why sponsor?

- Keep the control plane maintained (KubeVirt, Multus, Gateway API)
- Prioritize integrations you care about (within agreed scope)
- Support documentation and reproducible deployment tooling
- Back an independent Kubernetes-native IaaS alternative

### Custom / Corporate

For **SLA**, on-prem install support, custom features, or training:

- Contact the maintainer via GitHub Issues (label: `sponsor`) or email listed on the org profile
- Scope, timeline, and price defined in a **separate services agreement**
- Still no transfer of OSS copyright unless explicitly negotiated

## What sponsors can expect

- Transparency about how time is spent (releases, docs, integration testing)
- Professional conduct and clear boundaries (support ≠ product management)
- No guarantee that every feature request will be implemented

## What we ask from sponsors

- Respect Apache 2.0 and contributor community norms
- Do not imply official endorsement without approval
- Understand that public code remains open; paid work may target private branches or consulting deliverables only when contracted

## FAQ

**Can a sponsor fork and compete?**  
Yes — Apache 2.0 allows that. Sponsorship supports the **official** project, not exclusivity.

**Can I pay for a private fork?**  
Possible under a custom contract; the public `main` branch and tagged releases stay Apache 2.0 unless you negotiate a different arrangement for new work.

**Does sponsoring buy merge rights?**  
No. Influence is through communication and optional priority, not repository admin.

**Is this tax advice?**  
No. Consult your accountant for sponsorship vs. invoice treatment in your jurisdiction.
